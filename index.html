ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

Jiyun Lim

학생 사진 준비중

I am a student who has continued to explore biology by reading, asking questions, and connecting ideas across different areas of research.

This archive began as a way to keep track of what I was learning. From Grade 9 foundations to Grade 12 independent inquiry, my notes gradually developed from basic concepts and curiosity into paper reviews, critical analysis, research questions, hypothesis notes, and research proposals.

My interests include biomedical science, molecular and cellular biology, genetics and genomics, biotechnology, immunology, and infectious disease. Rather than simply collecting biological information, I use this archive to follow how one question leads to another and how evidence can change the way I think.

The goal is to document the process of learning itself — what I read, what I questioned, what I connected, and which ideas I would like to investigate further.
